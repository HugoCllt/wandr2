# WANDR — WEB APP DESIGN BRIEF (Laptop-First)

## Product Vision

Wandr is a modern web platform for discovering local activities in Montreal.  
It should feel intelligent, premium, visual, and effortless to use.

The experience combines the clarity and refinement of Apple with the warmth, lifestyle inspiration, and exploration energy of Airbnb.

Users should feel:
- inspired to go out
- curious to explore
- confident in choices
- able to decide quickly

This is not an event listing website.  
It is a curated discovery platform.

---

# Core Design Principles

## 1. Web-first laptop experience

Design for desktop/laptop first.

The interface should take advantage of horizontal space, hover states, multi-column layouts, side navigation, and richer browsing patterns.

Avoid mobile-app-stretched-to-web patterns.

---

## 2. Premium minimalism

The UI must feel clean, calm, refined, and highly intentional.

Use:
- generous spacing
- clear hierarchy
- elegant typography
- soft rounded corners
- subtle depth
- restrained animations
- minimal clutter

Avoid:
- noisy layouts
- excessive labels
- overuse of icons
- aggressive gradients
- visual chaos

---

## 3. Exploration-first UX

The product should encourage browsing and discovery before commitment.

The interface must reward curiosity.

Users should naturally keep scrolling, clicking, and opening activities.

---

# Visual Identity

## Color Palette

### Primary Background
Warm beige with subtle orange undertone.

### Secondary Accent
Signature orange for actions, highlights, selected states.

### Contrast Accent
Deep black used sparingly for structure, premium contrast, text emphasis.

### Neutral Support
Soft whites and muted grays.

---

## Typography

Use modern sans-serif typography.

Style direction:
- elegant
- readable
- premium
- calm
- slightly editorial

Hierarchy should be strong and obvious.

---

## Surfaces & Components

Use:
- rounded cards
- layered surfaces
- soft shadows
- clean borders
- premium spacing

Hover states should feel polished and subtle.

# Home Sections

## 1. Hero Carousel (Top)

Title examples:

Large immersive cards with strong visuals.

Examples:
- Canadiens game
- Formula 1 weekend
- Jazz night
- Rooftop event

---

## 2. Integrated Map Section

Clean embedded map showing nearby recommended activities.

Map should feel elegant and readable.

Pins prioritized visually to avoid clutter.

Selecting a pin highlights matching card.

---

## 3. Discovery Grid

Mixed structured layout using only 3 card types:

### Hero Horizontal Card
For premium/high-interest events.

### Standard Vertical Card
Default browsing card.

### Compact Row Card
Dense utility rows for secondary items.

Layout must feel curated, not random.

# Activity Cards

Every card includes:

- image
- title
- distance
- price
- date
- quick location button
- save/favorite button
- trend flame indicator

Optional:
- category tag
- limited spots
- discount badge

---

# Trend Flame Indicator

Signature Wandr metric.

A flame icon partially filled based on activity momentum.

Low flame:
quiet / niche / hidden gem

Medium flame:
healthy interest

Full flame:
popular

Super flame:
major trend / high demand

Should feel premium and intuitive.

---
